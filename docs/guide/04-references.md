# 4.0 参考文献

本页列出用户指南中最常用的 EPA 文件和 AERMOD 技术文献。完整书目以英文原版第 4 章为准。

1. **AECOM, 2010.** AERMOD Low Wind Speed Evaluation Study results. AECOM Environment, Westford, Massachusetts.
2. **API, 2013.** Ambient Ratio Method Version 2 (ARM2) for use with AERMOD for 1-hour NO₂ Modeling: Development and Evaluation Report. American Petroleum Institute, Washington, DC.
3. **EPA, 2017b.** *Guideline on Air Quality Models*, Appendix W to 40 CFR Part 51.
4. **EPA, 2018.** *User's Guide for the AERMOD Terrain Preprocessor (AERMAP)*. EPA-454/B-18-004.
5. **EPA, 2021.** *AERSCREEN User's Guide*. EPA-454/B-21-005.
6. **EPA, 2023a.** *AERMOD Model Formulation*. EPA-454/B-23-010.
7. **EPA, 2023b.** *AERMOD Implementation Guide*. EPA-454/B-23-009.
8. **EPA, 2023c.** *User's Guide for the AERMOD Meteorological Preprocessor (AERMET)*. EPA-454/B-23-005.
9. **EPA, 2023d.** *Incorporation and Evaluation of the RLINE Source Type in AERMOD for Mobile Source Applications*. EPA-2023/R-23-011.
10. **Cimorelli, A. J., et al., 2005.** AERMOD: A dispersion model for industrial source applications. Part I: General model formulation and boundary layer characterization. *Journal of Applied Meteorology*, 44, 682–693.
11. **Perry, S. G., et al., 2005.** AERMOD: A dispersion model for industrial source applications. Part II: Model performance against 17 field study databases. *Journal of Applied Meteorology*, 44, 694–708.
12. **Schulman, L. L. and Scire, J. S., 1980.** Buoyant Line and Point Source Dispersion Model (BLP).
13. **Venkatram, A., et al., 2013.** Meteorological inputs for AERMOD: estimating surface friction velocity in low wind speed stable conditions.
